How the engine decides

The engine optimises for cash generation and capital productivity first, with inventory clearance as the last resort. Seven metrics are each scored 1–5, then blended with type-specific weights into a composite. Priority order: cash generation → capital productivity → demand quality → margin protection → clearance.

Seasonal weights Sell-through 25% · Seasonal 25% · Capital productivity 15% · Cash velocity 10% · Coverage 10% · Age 10% · Financing 5%.
Future seasonal demand still leads, but capital efficiency now counts.
Evergreen weights Capital productivity 25% · Sell-through 20% · Coverage 20% · Cash velocity 15% · Age 10% · Seasonal 5% · Financing 5%.
No season to wait for, so capital productivity dominates.

The three capital metrics

  • Capital productivity (CPI) = gross profit generated ÷ (COGS invested × age in years). Above 1.0 scores 5; below 0.25 scores 1. A SKU that converts its tied-up capital into profit quickly is protected from markdowns even if sell-through is slow.
  • Cash velocity = profit per piece ÷ (age days + collection days). Rewards SKUs that return cash fast; ₹3+/day scores 5, under ₹0.6/day scores 1.
  • Financing pressure = financing cost per piece ÷ profit per piece. Under 10% scores 5; over 50% scores 1. A profitable SKU that ties up capital too long is pulled down.

Margin-aware markdown ceiling

Static discount tiers are gone. The safe ceiling = (profit ÷ price) × 70%, so a markdown can never wipe out the unit's profit. Small/Medium/Aggressive scale that ceiling at ⅓ / ⅔ / full. Coverage now uses demand-weighted sales (70% last-30-days, 30% last-90-day average) to react faster to shifts.

Capital lock

Flags any SKU over 270 days old with 4+ months of coverage and shows the rupee value (stock × COGS) trapped in slow inventory.

Overrides

  • Maturity gate (cold-start): a SKU isn't discounted until it's had a fair selling window. Its first 90 days are always protected (the freshest age tier), extendable via the launch grace period for long manufacture-to-shelf lags, and a seasonal piece is also protected while its major season is still ahead. In all these cases it shows a “building read” status instead of a markdown — low sell-through and high coverage on a freshly produced line are artifacts of newness, not weak demand. The gate only steps in when the engine would otherwise have discounted; a new SKU already selling well still reads as Maintain.
  • Seasonal fatigue: if windows passed ≥ 2 and sell-through < 50%, the composite is cut by a full point and the action is floored at Medium markdown — the market has seen the design too many times.
  • Age override (seasonal only): if 0 windows have passed and the major season is still ahead, inventory age is set aside so an "old-but-pre-season" design isn't punished for calendar days.

Action bands (after overrides)

  • ≥ 4.50 Maintain price · 0%
  • 4.00–4.49 Monitor · 0%
  • 3.00–3.99 Small markdown · ⅓ of safe ceiling
  • 2.00–2.99 Medium markdown · ⅔ of safe ceiling
  • < 2.00 Aggressive markdown · full safe ceiling · consider liquidation
